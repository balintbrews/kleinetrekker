I’m Bálint, and I’m the second owner of this car. In our family, we call it Little Tractor (“Kis Traktor” in Hungarian, “Kleine Trekker” in Dutch).

Its first owner bought it new in Sweden. After he passed away, his brother brought the car to Hungary and put it up for sale. I bought it there in February 2022 with 68,000 km on the clock, and I’ve felt lucky to look after it ever since. When my wife and I moved to the Netherlands in 2024, the car came with us.

For a long time, it was our hobby car. We’d take it out on the occasional weekend. It’s our daily driver now, though it only gets light use. With less time and energy these days, I’d like it to get a little more care than I can give it. I’d love for it to go to someone who’ll look after it and feel as happy as we were when we brought it home.

When I bought the car, both bumpers were repainted. Roadster Service (Lalee), a familiar name among Hungarian MX-5 owners, fitted a new clutch and handled all the maintenance the car needed while it was in Hungary. Since we moved to the Netherlands, MX5sports has taken care of it.

What I’d take care of next

Catalytic converter
The catalytic converter in the center exhaust pipe needs to be replaced. You can hear a hissing sound at higher revs.
Head unit and controls
The Pioneer is starting to act up, and I think it’s reaching the end of its life. Its remote has also been unreliable for a while, so the audio buttons on the steering wheel don’t always respond as they should. I’d replace the head unit and remote and go with wireless CarPlay this time.
Right door speaker
Ever since I bought the car, this speaker has distorted in wet or humid weather. It sounds normal again once it dries out. I’ve had it looked at, but the cause is still a mystery.
Seats and upholstery
The leather on both seats still looks really good overall, except for one worn spot where the driver’s seat belt rubs against it. I’d have that spot repaired. The driver’s seat also squeaks, and I think the springs underneath need to be replaced.
Driver’s seat belt
The belt is slow to retract and needs to be guided back by hand.
